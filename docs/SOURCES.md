# Research and supplied assets

The four summaries in arrays.xml are original paraphrases of the following official sources. They are ordered Beijing, Shanghai, Chongqing, Tianjin, following the written requirement (the sample in the brief reverses the last two).

- Beijing: [Facts](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/beijinginfo/abstract/202005/t20200512_1895468.html), [History and geography](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/beijinginfo/facts/), [Imperial palace](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/beijinginfo/culture/culturecalendar/information/202401/t20240112_3534073.html).
- Shanghai: [Welcome to Shanghai](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Overview/20231212/c9aa0f22df154557bbbbb30d996eb2b2.html), [Historical outline](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Overview/20231209/05cfb15755fe4cea80a0d3a1d69512e2.html), [Shanghai Basic Facts](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/assets/Download/2022-ShanghaiBasicFacts.pdf).
- Chongqing: [Geography](https://english.cq.gov.cn/aboutchongqing/overview/Geography/), [Jiefangbei and municipal history](https://english.cq.gov.cn/specials/explore/PhotoPerfectSpots/202608/t20260810_15912652.html).
- Tianjin: [Historical origins and geographical setting](https://en.tj.gov.cn/tianjininfo/profile/202312/t20231225_6489676.html).

Research date: 21 October 2026. The photographs, map, and app logo were supplied in the Practical 5 course resource pack. The provided map is a PNG despite the brief referring to a JPG. Adaptive and legacy icon sizes are derived from the supplied app_logo.png; no external image assets were added.
